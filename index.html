The Problem

Most home
espresso is guesswork.

You buy a great machine. You buy great beans. And every morning, the same questions: How fine should I grind? Why was it sour today? Why didn't it pull like yesterday? Voltresso answers all of them — for your machine, your bag, your kitchen.

  • No more guessing on dose
    Machine-calibrated brew guides tell you exactly how much coffee, how much water, what temperature — to the gram and second.
  • Diagnose any shot, instantly
    Sour? Bitter? Channelling? No crema? Tap your symptom and Volt explains exactly what to adjust on your specific machine.
  • Get measurably better, every week
    Every shot you log builds a personal brew history. Voltresso spots the patterns, surfaces your trends, and tells you what's improving.

Questions

Frequently
asked.

What is Voltresso?
Voltresso is an espresso brewing companion. It gives you machine-tuned brew guides, real-time shot diagnosis from Voltbot, and a complete library of coffee origins, recipes, and brewing techniques — all in one beautifully designed app.
Who built Voltresso?
Voltresso is built by a passionate barista who loves every kind of coffee — espresso, Turkish, pour-over, Moka, whatever the recipe. Years of pulling shots, reading, studying, and brewing every single day went into this app, with one goal: make it easier for anyone to make great coffee.
Which espresso machines does Voltresso support?
100+ machines across every category — single boiler, dual boiler, heat exchanger, lever, super-automatic, capsule, Moka pot, and Turkish cezve. Each one has a brew guide calibrated to its specific characteristics, so the advice you get is actually relevant.
How does Voltbot work?
Voltbot comes in two tiers. The free tier is a chatbot built right into the app — it's aware of everything you're doing inside Voltresso (your machine, your beans, your shot history) and helps you accordingly. The pro tier is connected to a Claude API, giving you Claude as your personal artificial intelligence barista for unlimited deep-dive conversations.
When is Voltresso launching?
We're working hard on Voltresso and hope to launch as soon as possible. Follow us on Instagram or Facebook for the announcement.
Does Voltresso work without internet?
Most of Voltresso works fully offline — brew guides, recipes, bean library, shot logging. Internet is required for device scanning of bean bags and for the pro tier of Voltbot.
Will Voltresso connect to my smart espresso machine?
Direct connectivity for Bluetooth-enabled machines is under consideration for a future release. We can't promise it yet, but it's on the roadmap.
